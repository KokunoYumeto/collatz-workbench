# Snapshot scope

This snapshot exposes the locally maintained mathematical manuscripts, canonical PDFs,
proof and finite-check code, claim/morphism/source records, and selected mathematical
audits. It preserves separate programmes and rejected history instead of flattening
them into one apparent theorem. No new mathematics or build was performed to publish it.

Original mathematical text is unchanged. Host-account names in public text copies are
replaced by LOCAL_USER. MIRROR_MANIFEST.json records both original and public hashes;
older embedded receipts and manifests refer to their original packages, not necessarily
these privacy-filtered copies. Scripts that consult the private corpus need equivalent
local source paths. Portable manuscript packages have their own build instructions.

Not uploaded: private raw conversations, credentials or browser sessions, third-party
paper/book scans and source collections, caches, scratch page renders, and duplicate
temporary build trees. Source identities and citations remain available. The Maxwell
edition comes from its existing portable release, expanded into browsable files.

Historical mentions of publication being unauthorized describe older local states;
the present GitHub publication was directly requested on 14 September 2026.
The research_program root quarantine concerns its old root chapters; the separately
dated split_zero_history_20260913 note is the later independently developed work.

No blanket license is imposed on third-party work. Source authors retain their
attributions. This repository records a human–LLM research collaboration under the
public name Kokuno Yumeto; the recorded LLM was ChatGPT 5.6 Sol, Ultra mode, in Codex.
