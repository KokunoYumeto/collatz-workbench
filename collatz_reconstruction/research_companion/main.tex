\begin{abstract}
We retain the positive-odd first-return coordinates of the Collatz map and
construct their legal block category, coefficientwise path completion,
predecessor transfer operator, injective decorated prefix embedding, and
finite residue transformation semigroups.  Exact comparison maps separate
two $\mathbb F_2^2$ gradings, the prefix coordinate from the forward affine
intercept, and forward actions from contravariant pullback.  The finite
semigroups receive constructive normal forms, cardinalities, ranks, minimum
ideals, reduction fibres, and a coefficient-level $3$-adic inverse limit.

The same prefix pieces define a Gaussian relation lattice for rational
arctangent angles.  We construct its exact signed-valuation morphism, prove an
all-word non-torsion theorem for the unweighted sum, determine the exceptional
two-letter lattice, and give a coefficient-unbounded exact certificate for
every prefix of length at most fifteen and cumulative exponent at most sixty.
The resulting all-length rigidity statement is isolated as a conjecture and
crosswalked against the published Gaussian and Machin literature.

The finite prefix classes then give a homeomorphism from the full countable
label shift to the odd $2$-adic survivor space obtained by deleting the
backward orbit of $-1/3$.  We locate the positive-integer image, prove its
canonical-representative stabilization criterion, and compute the exact
full-shift pressure.  Finally, object-idempotent and formal-scheme arguments
give precise boundaries for proposed tropical and prismatic interfaces.

The same path coordinates define a discrete arithmetic orbit groupoid with
two exact cocycles and a faithful path-algebra embedding.  Exact
homeomorphisms of $\mathbb Z_3$ generate a compact-unit germ groupoid, and a
proved affine-germ functor carries each arithmetic orbit arrow into it while
retaining its bidegree and real weight.  A unimodular torus map then turns the
bidegree into the arithmetic character, yielding commutative $1$-motive
squares, Kummer periods, a Cayley isomorphism of algebraic pairs, and the
contravariant Nori edges induced by actual pair maps.

Finally, positive exponent compositions are identified exactly with pointed
shortened-map parity words.  The resulting groupoid isomorphism carries packet
rotation to rotation by the corresponding exponent prefix, and the classical
rational-cycle numerator becomes the odd-step affine numerator without a
change of convention.  The primitive packet of length $d$ and exponent sum
$B$ has exact periods $d$, $B$, and $B+d$ under the odd-return, shortened,
and full maps, respectively.  A finite-state residue recursion and two independent
integer-only scans certify all strip-selected packets through odd-step period
sixteen, including the complete $99$-class period-eight table.
The Chinese remainder coordinate map intertwines the residue transitions
letter by letter.  Its synchronized product retains the common exponent
word, whereas marginalization has an explicit rectangle-basis kernel.
The complete length-ten, sum-sixteen residue table separates primitive
words from repeated words and records their different divisor exclusions.

The packet affine offsets also admit a closed two-state generating family.
The corresponding fixed-point series is uniquely characterized
coefficientwise by a dyadic dilation equation.  We determine its exact
Taylor domains, construct a normally convergent meromorphic continuation,
and compute every finite pole, its order, and its leading coefficient.

Every nonconjectural Collatz-specific assertion is proved in the paper;
portable finite certificates independently check the bounded algebra,
residue, symbolic, pressure, Gaussian-relation, groupoid, affine-germ,
torus-character, and pair-map calculations.  The results do not assert
termination of all positive Collatz orbits.
\end{abstract}
