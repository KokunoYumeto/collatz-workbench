\section{The current mathematical boundary}

The preceding sections prove finite-word affine transport, exact descent and
coalescence criteria, a restricted predecessor-semigroup law, and the stated
finite certificate. No conjecture about the unrestricted recursive generator
is advanced here. In particular, the fact that
Certificate~\ref{cert:finite-k34} stops at $k=34$ does not itself define an
open problem and supplies no evidence for an unbounded invariant.

The inequality
\[
 2^{t(w)}3^{c-d(w)}\le 2^s
\]
is the necessary and sufficient high-coordinate condition in
Theorem~\ref{thm:recursive-lift-criterion}; it is not asserted for words or
residues outside the proved finite domain. Before any unrestricted statement
can be formulated, the generator must supply a mathematically defined set of
admissible tuples
\[
 (P,r,s,c,d(w),t(w),H(w))
\]
independent of mutable program state. Until that domain exists and is proved
to represent the intended recursion, there is neither a theorem nor a
conjecture here.

The exact cylinder-period question formerly listed in this chapter is solved
by the finite translation-stabilizer construction in
Theorem~\ref{thm:general-branch-cylinder}; retaining it as an open item would
be false. Source acquisition, literature crosswalking, witness-format design,
and formalization are project work recorded outside the mathematical argument,
not mathematical open problems.
