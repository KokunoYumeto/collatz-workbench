This is the separately authored mathematical workbench. It is not the
source-faithful critical reader.

Compile `main.tex` with pdfLaTeX. Read the files in this order:

1. `chapters/00_status_and_scope.tex` — claim types and nonclaims.
2. `chapters/01_residue_cylinders.tex` — the general branch-cylinder theorem.
3. `chapters/02_dyadic_uniformity.tex` — exact dyadic lifts, descent, fibres,
   and coalescence.
4. `chapters/03_recursive_sieve_semigroup.tex` — the restricted predecessor
   semigroup and the finite `k <= 34` coefficient theorem.
5. `chapters/04_research_programme.tex` — the exact current boundary between
   proved mathematics and work that has not earned mathematical status.

`PROVED` means the displayed statement has a proof in this document.
`FINITE CERTIFICATE` means a mathematical reduction plus a pinned exhaustive
finite check at the stated bound. A missing proof removes or downgrades a
claim; it is not relabelled as an obligation. Tasks are not open problems, and
no conjecture is admitted without an exact statement justified by the
preceding mathematics or identified literature.
