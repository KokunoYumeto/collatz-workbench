# Collatz histories through support and Hurwitz zero clusters

Live mathematical source: `note.tex`. This is a separate research-program note, not an attribution to Maxwell Siegel or an alteration of his critical edition.

The note constructs two compatible finite interfaces from primary Split Zero sources:

1. A forward affine polynomial whose coefficients recover a finite Collatz parity word, including its zero positions when length is retained. Specialization and support reflection have explicitly computed information loss.
2. Exact odd-return residue coordinates, followed by the compact Hurwitz transform. A zero-cluster jet of order K−1 recovers every weight on a known K-word packet and hence every synchronized residue predicate. All maps and inverses are displayed. The jet order is sharp for recovery of the full unrestricted probability vector.

The application is a proved finite transport of information. It is not a descent estimate, a proof of RH or Collatz, a numerical stability theorem, or a claim of priority over the literature. The coefficients/weights are already input to the constructed family; the spectral reconstruction does not obtain unknown orbit data for free.

Primary source identities, hashes, checks, and publication receipts are recorded alongside the note. `check_history.py` is an exact rational finite certificate, not a formalization of complex analysis. No Lean build is required.
