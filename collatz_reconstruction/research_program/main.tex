\begin{abstract}
This document is the separately authored research layer accompanying a
source-faithful Collatz critical reader.  It develops exact residue-cylinder
coordinates for piecewise-affine integer maps, proves the resulting uniform
descent and coalescence criteria, and isolates a finite theorem validating the
recursive high-coordinate test used by a public $2^{34}$ sieve generator.
It also proves that a printed generalized-map coordinate cannot be repaired by
silently reducing modulo its displayed denominator. Every finite computation
is stated at its exact certified scope; the point at which the current finite
theorem stops is not promoted to a conjecture merely because further
mathematics remains to be done.
\end{abstract}
