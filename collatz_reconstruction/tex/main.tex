\begin{abstract}
This live edition reconstructs Collatz and adjacent mathematics from primary
literature and an authorized local corpus.  It separates sourced theorems,
independent deductions, finite calculations, definitions, choices, analogies,
conjectures, missing arrows, contradictions, retractions, and nonclaims.  The
document remains incomplete until its external coverage, claim, dependency,
morphism, proof, formalization, and recovery ledgers all pass their release
gates.
\end{abstract}
