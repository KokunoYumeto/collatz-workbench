\section{Scope, authority, and exactness}

\subsection{Subject gate}

The subject is the Collatz problem and mathematics joined to it by an explicit
mathematical edge.  An edge must be exhibited by a common typed object, an
explicit map, a theorem whose hypotheses include an admitted Collatz object,
or a proved reduction or obstruction.  Shared vocabulary, analogous diagrams,
and co-occurrence in a local folder do not suffice.

The zeta project supplied a workflow pattern and a frozen routing index.  It
supplies no mathematical zeta--Collatz claim.  Any such claim, if encountered,
must pass the same typed-map and exact-proof gate as every other proposed
connection.

\subsection{Source order}

Published literature is read first and has no invented admission threshold.
Primary source \TeX{} controls when it is available; a PDF is read when it is
the sole or controlling witness.  Modern local Chatnotes are reconstructed
only after the relevant literature has fixed definitions and known results.
Residual Gemini-science material is read last as unverified intake.  Its
correct bounded results may survive independent proof, but its attribution or
rhetorical conclusion supplies no evidence.

\subsection{Typed records}

Each mathematical record receives a stable identifier and one of the following
roles: sourced theorem, independent theorem, calculation, definition, choice,
analogy, conjecture, missing arrow, impossibility result, contradiction,
retraction, or explicit nonclaim.  These distinctions are epistemic and do not
assert that the underlying mathematics is disconnected.

For every map the edition records its domain, codomain, coordinate formula,
well-definedness, preserved operations or relations, kernel or fibres where
applicable, injectivity and surjectivity status, inverse formula and identities
where applicable, exceptional locus, information loss, hypotheses, and proof
dependencies.  A claimed nonexistence is admitted only with an explicit
obstruction.

\subsection{Propagation rule}

When a source correction, counterexample, repaired proof, or new bounded result
changes a dependent statement, the change is propagated through the live
text, claim and dependency records, morphism ledger, programme reconstruction,
formal certificates, exact gap records, and action receipts before any later
consequence is used.

\subsection{Current status}

This is a live artifact.  At the present checkpoint it fixes method and scope
only; it does not yet assert content-level literature or local-corpus coverage,
and it makes no claim about the Collatz conjecture's endpoint.
